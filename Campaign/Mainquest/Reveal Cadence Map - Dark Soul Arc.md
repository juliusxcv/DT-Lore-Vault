# Reveal Cadence Map — The Dark Soul Arc

> **GM eyes only.** The pacing instrument for the slow-burn reveal that the Midnight Wraith holds a Shard of the Dark Soul, and that the Dark Mechanicus intends to complete it as a new Chaos god.

**The three tiers (revelation rationed by proximity):**
- **TEXTURE** — atmospheric, deniable, multiple meanings, no thread connecting clues to each other. Players file it under known 40K phenomena.
- **ANOMALY** — "this does not fit anything we know." The 40K framework visibly strains. Still unnamed.
- **NAME** — clues that can actually identify the true nature (Dark Soul, Apostles, the cyclical entity, the Ancient Enemy, a new god).

**The one rule under all of it:** every clue carries a *cover reading* (a plausible 40K explanation) and a *true reading*. Players spend their deduction budget on the cover. Never drop a clue that has only one possible meaning until the Citadel.

**Tags used below:** `[GM RATIFY]` = invented/uncertain, needs your decision. `[DROPPED]` = already in play (Session ≤ III).

## Beat 1 — Lower levels *(months out; new table)*

**Tier: TEXTURE → first ANOMALY.**

- **Glyphs nothing can classify** — not Chaos runes, not any logged xenos script. *(Texture: "unknown archaeotech." True: Apostolic script.)*
- **Reanimation too clean** — bodies repaired with non-Imperial genetic precision, not rotted-and-risen. *First "this isn't standard Chaos undead" beat — still deniable as xeno-archaeotech.*
- **Harridan's cipher** on redacted high-tier Mechanicus logs. *(Cover: classified Inquisitorial business. True: his Dark Soul experiments.)*
- **First Dark Mechanicus sign** — sabotage at Epsilon, a shrine, servitors reciting scrambled non-Omnissiah litanies. *(Cover: tech-heresy. True: a cult breaching containment.)*

**Guardrail:** the strangeness is "unknown xenos / archaeotech," NOT "from outside this universe." No name.

## Beat 2 — Finding Kill-team Velatum Umbra

**Tier: ANOMALY, escalating.**

- **They find the *scene*, not the survivor** — the team's last position, their downed Corvus *Blackstar*, the dead, the final logs. The one who lived (the Fallen Blackshield) is met later, in Beat 5. Beats 2 and 5 are the two ends of one thread.
- **Their final logs** — descent, time dilation, voices, "it speaks." Let an Astartes say plainly: *the psychic residue is inconsistent with warp exposure.* (Straight from your Velatum Umbra brief.) First hard "not the warp" data point.
- **Signs of betrayal from within** — a break in formation, a bulkhead sealed from the wrong side, one set of ceramite tracks leaving while the rest die in place. *(Cover: "a traitor, a Chaos turn." True: the Blackshield broke ranks — seeds Beat 5 without naming him.)*
- **Drukhari spoor** — the small hunting party that finished them. *(Cover: "xenos got them." True: they only got the opening because the Blackshield derailed.)*
- **The Mask thread deepens** for Vex in parallel.

**Guardrail:** name the *anomaly category* (non-warp, non-known-xenos) and let them suspect a traitor — but do not reveal the Blackshield's identity or nature here. Save the face for Beat 5.

> **Continuity note — the Drukhari.** Your World Lore's "Shards of Venom" raid, with its Blackshield-led Deathwatch clash, is the *first* engagement (Deathwatch dominant; half the Drukhari fall). The "Pursuers" splinter (Vaelinth + elite warriors) shadows the kill-team deeper; the Fallen's derailment opens the *second*, fatal engagement found here. Same Blackshield, same Fallen, same green angel as Beat 5. *(Holds pending your confirmation of the sequencing.)*

## Beat 3 — Lowest level of Tenebris

**Tier: ANOMALY peaks; first NAME fragments — but unreliable.**

- **Harridan's research logs** finally open. His own words: the cyclical entity, the Apostles, Rift-Travel, reanimation of dead tissue. The *concept* surfaces here — but as a missing, radical inquisitor's possibly-mad theory, never confirmed fact.
- **The forbidden cosmology, stated once:** the salt-and-ash seal, the Ancient Enemy, "a primordial force fractured by the emergence of Light." First time the truth is spoken aloud — framed as unreliable pre-Imperial text.
- **The Dark Mechanicus plan turns legible:** not scavengers. They are trying to *free* and *complete* it.

**Guardrail:** players can now read the words "Dark Soul" and "Apostles," but it must still feel like one dead man's heresy, not settled truth. (See MC-veteran note — this is where veterans hit near-certainty; keep newer players merely intrigued.)

**Tier: NAME, confirmation building.**

## Beat 5 — The ships + the fallen green angel

**Tier: NAME + the set-piece where the two cosmologies visibly collide.**

**Nature (ratified):** a Dark Angels Space Marine — one of the Fallen — fleeing his Chapter. He rode with Velatum Umbra as a Blackshield; deep in the facility, when the Pursuers struck, his own agenda made him break ranks, and the kill-team died for it. He is the survivor the players have been circling since Beat 2.

- **Standard hulk enemies (Genestealers, etc.)** — deliberate breather; reinforces "ordinary space hulk" right before the un-ordinary core.

| Clue / lever | Cover reading | True reading / use |
|---|---|---|
| Green ceramite, no chapter markings | "A Fallen — what chapter, why hiding, is he a traitor?" | His secret is the *Dark Angels'* secret, not the Dark Soul — a parallel mystery that eats their deduction budget. Free misdirection. |
| He knows what happened to Velatum Umbra | Closes the missing-team mystery from Beat 2 | Confirms the deep horror, the time/space distortion, the "not warp" — **anomaly tier, not name** |
| His guilt / evasiveness | "He's lying, he turned" | He got them killed and knows it — a trust-and-roleplay lever for the party |
| What he glimpsed near the artifact | A haunted veteran's half-truths | He saw enough to dread it, not enough to name it — or won't say |

**Guardrail — the hard one:** he is your strongest confirmation lever, so gate him. He may confirm the *anomaly* (the team's fate, the descent, that it is not the warp) but he must **not name the Dark Soul, the Apostles, or the ritual.** Naming stays sealed until Beat 6. If he knows more than he says, his silence *is* the dread.

**Cross-link:** Beat 2 is the crime scene; Beat 5 is the confession. Don't let the party meet him until they've stood where his team fell.

Ask veterans out-of-game to sit on what they suspect; most enjoy being co-conspirators of dread.
