# Scene: The Answer

### Hollowkin Resonance Contact — Vex Solo Beat

**Type:** Roleplay / Psychic encounter — no combat intended **Placement:** Flexible. Best run at dusk near Theta Gate, before or shortly after the Shadow Sentinel encounter. Works as a solo beat for Vex, or semi-solo with one other PC shadowing her. **Reveal Tier:** Texture, with one calibrated Anomaly spike (see Calibration Notes) **Pays off:** "Answer to Vex Spell — Call into the Forest"

---

## Purpose (GM-Side)

- Answer Vex's psychic call with a contact that _feels_ like an answer but delivers no names.
- Seed the arrival of the _Midnight Wraith_ as pure sensory memory — the players' first eyewitness account of the campaign's central secret, delivered by a witness who cannot speak.
- Establish the Hollowkin as the moon's living warning system (their camouflage hides them _from the shard_, not from predators). [NEW CANON]
- Plant the **"symmetry"** Texture clue for MC veterans (see Calibration Notes).
- Optionally touch the Mask thread — both versions provided, **[GM RATIFY]** before running.

## Player-Facing Objective

Vex follows the pull of something answering her call. That's all she knows.

---

## Staging & Atmosphere

The contact comes at dusk, when the **Evening Spores** rise and lay a shimmering haze over the forest floor. Use the native flora as instruments — this is the Hollowkin's "voice" arriving before the Hollowkin does:

- The **Echoleaf** rustles without wind — but in _rhythm_, like breathing.
- A distant **Singing Tree** begins its low hum — out of season, out of alignment.
- **Whisperbloom** flowers open along Vex's path though no eclipse is due, vibrating faintly, marking a trail deeper into the pines.
- The **Silent Moss** underfoot swallows her footsteps entirely. She realizes she cannot hear herself walking. The forest has muted her.

> _The light here doesn't fall. It crawls. And tonight, it is crawling toward something that is waiting for you._

**Table mechanic — peripheral vision:** The Hollowkin is visible only when _not_ looked at. Whenever the player says Vex looks directly at it, describe empty forest. Describe the Hollowkin only when she looks away — at the edge of her sight, taller than a man, thin, oil-and-glass. If the player catches on and deliberately keeps her gaze averted, reward it: the contact begins.

---

## The Approach

The Hollowkin does not move toward her. The _distance_ simply becomes less, between blinks. When it stands within ten paces (always peripheral, never centered), the vibration starts — felt in the sternum, the teeth, the long bones. Not words. Pressure that _means_.

If Vex opens herself to it (player choice — no test required to begin, this is consensual on both sides):

## The Transmission

Run as five movements. Read each aloud, then pause — let the player react between movements. Vex experiences these as _being there_, not watching.

### Movement I — The Hum Before

> Green-gold depth. You are roots and soil and the slow pulse of the forest's one long thought. There is twilight, always twilight, and the twilight is _kind_. We are many — dozens, hundreds — moving through the pines, and we are not hiding. We sing through the ground to one another, openly, brightly. The moon hums one chord, and everything alive is part of it.
### Movement II — The Arrival

> The chord breaks — but nothing falls. There is no fire, no descent, no warning in the sky. Between one pulse of the song and the next, the stone simply _contains something_. A vastness of black that was not there before, and then was — folded into the deep rock as though it had always been, except the moon's whole body flinches around it like flesh around a splinter. We feel it strain — once, twice — against an _elsewhere_. Against a current that pulls at it, hungry to carry it away. It refuses the current. It anchors. And then — silence. A silence with a shape. A silence shaped like the thing beneath, waiting.

_(The_ Midnight Wraith _materialized directly inside the moon — rift travel, not warp translation. The shard fights the warp's pull, so the hulk is anchored in realspace, trapped in stone with nowhere to go but to wait. Never name "rift travel" at the table; the players get only the sensation of arrival-without-transit. The "current it refuses" is the warp — let psyker-minded players chew on a ship that resists the warp instead of riding it.)

_ [NEW CANON — Hollowkin predate and witnessed the arrival. **Timeline flag:** the memory implies materialization predates the M38.991 Imperial survey; ratify against your hulk chronology.]

### Movement III — The Pattern

> For a long time the buried thing only sleeps. Then it begins to _dream outward_. You feel it the way you'd feel a second heartbeat under your own — almost yours, almost right. Its song is beautiful. Every note answered, every line mirrored, a pattern of such perfect symmetry that nothing living could have made it. The trees that hear it too long grow in straight lines. Those of us who listen too long begin to sing _back_.


### Movement IV — The Dimming

> We learn the only defense there is: to be quiet in the way that matters. Not silence of sound — silence of _presence_. We fold our light inward. We unmake our faces, our voices, our reflections, until even the dreaming thing's perfect pattern cannot find an edge of us to grasp. This is not how we hide from beasts. Beasts were never the danger. We dimmed ourselves so that _it_ would stop singing to us.


### Movement V — The Warning

> The memory releases you, and the pressure turns — toward _you_, present-tense, personal. It moves through your bones like a hand passed over a candle flame, measuring:
> 
> **You burn too bright. It has already heard you. When you go down into the dark, fold your light — or its song will learn your name.**

Then the Hollowkin shows her — once, slowly, as pure sensation — _how to dim_. A folding-inward of the soul. Whether Vex can ever reproduce it is up to you.

_(Optional mechanical payoff: a one-use technique — **"The Dimming"** — Vex may attempt once, later, to mask her psychic presence from the shard's attention at a critical moment. Costly, draining, single charge. Keep it in your pocket for the lower levels or the Wraith itself.)_ [NEW CANON — GM RATIFY]

## Resolution & Aftermath

The contact ends when the GM chooses — ideally mid-breath. Vex blinks, and the forest is only forest. The Whisperblooms are closed. Her footsteps make sound again.

**What Vex can report to the party** (player-facing summary, deliberately lossy):

- Something native, ancient, and _afraid_ answered her — afraid of what's below, not of the Imperium.
- It showed her something huge appearing _inside_ the moon, long ago — there between one heartbeat and the next, never having fallen, and trapped ever since.
- The thing below fights against being _taken somewhere_ — it holds itself here, deliberately.
- It warned her that whatever is down there can _hear_ psykers.
- It hides not from people, but from _that_.

**What does not translate:** the symmetry. Vex felt it, but human language reduces it to "the song was too perfect." Let that phrase be exactly what she says at the table.

## Complications (choose 0–2)

- **The Marksman's Scope.** Kael Ryn or another Sentinel patrol has eyes on the clearing. A las-dot settles on the distortion beside Vex. One round of frantic player diplomacy — or a dead first contact and a forest that goes _cold_ toward the party permanently.
- **A Shadow at Her Back.** Another PC followed her. They see nothing — only Vex, swaying, speaking to empty air with her nose bleeding. How they report this shapes party trust.
- **Valeria Hears of It.** If word reaches the Lord Inquisitor: she is already unsettled by Vex, and unsanctioned xenos communion is exactly the heresy the Arbites report warned of. Her next correspondence to PSY-VEX sharpens noticeably — probing what was _exchanged_. (Strong Session-correspondence hook.)
- **The Echo.** For the next session, Vex occasionally hears the symmetry-song faintly through floors and walls — strongest near sealed shafts. A live clue-direction-finder the players will use, which is the point.

## Calibration Notes (Split Audience)

- **Newer players** receive: gothic first-contact, a buried _something_, a psyker warning. Reads as classic 40K warp/xenos mystery. The Eldar theory survives intact — tall, thin, faceless only _feeds_ it.
- **MC veterans** receive exactly one loaded word: **symmetry**, used twice in Movement III and never again. Do not embellish it, do not repeat it later in the session. If a veteran's eyebrow goes up, the Texture tier is working; if asked directly, the Hollowkin "had no word for it — only the shape."
- **Second veteran-keyed clue — the absent warp.** Vex is a psyker; she knows what warp translation _feels_ like, even secondhand. This memory contains none of it. The arrival felt like _nothing_ — and the thing actively strains against "a current that wants to take it." A vessel that fights the warp's pull rather than traveling by it is quietly impossible by every 40K framework the players own. Deliver it once, in Movement II, and let it lie there.
- **Do not** let the transmission show the hulk's interior, any humanoid figures of the Dark Legion, or anything dateable. The memory is geology and song.
