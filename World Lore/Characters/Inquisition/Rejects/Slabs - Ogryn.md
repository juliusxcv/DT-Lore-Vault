## GM Summary
- **Role:** Ogryn heavy — ripper gun, brute-force problem solving
- **Squad epithets:** the Dumb One (Thornkell)
- **Designations:** S148-Z is his primary designation (intake); OGR-271 is the Arbites case designation. Both are valid.
- **Ratified canon:** The Ork cranial implants described in the Arbites report are genuine — installed by an Ork Painboy during his mercenary service under Warboss Grommuk the Green. They account for his atypical tactical acumen. The intake questionnaire makes no mention of them.
- **Custody origin:** Captured by Imperial forces after Operation Final Stand; high-security containment under neural dampening fields
- **Active hooks:** Implant stability and ideological drift remain an open question; percussive cogitator "persuasion" now sanctioned only on Valeria's explicit order; notices things others miss (Rook's disappearances)

2b Incident Documentation:

Explain yourself! What prompted such heretical interaction? Were your actions driven by duty, survival, or some impulse? Provide a detailed account of the events, the race you encountered and the outcome. The Ordo Xenos does not tolerate ambiguity.

  

“Oi, Inquisitor! You want to know how an Ogryn like me ended up workin' for Ork pirates? Well, sit tight, 'cause it's a tale worth tellin'. After the Orks came crashin' down on my homeworld, I was left standin' amidst the wreckage, lookin' for a way to keep breathin'. They caught me, but instead of makin' me a prisoner, they dangled somethin' shiny in front of me: pay!

  

These Ork pirates, they ain't like the Imperium. They see strength and know how to reward it. They promised me a hefty sum of teef—gold, shiny bits, and all sorts of loot. I thought to meself, 'Slabs, you can smash heads and get paid for it? Sounds like a right good deal!' So, I signed up as a mercenary, servin' under the Ork Warboss, Grommuk the Green.

  

The pay was better than any Imperial guard could dream of. I was promised a share of the spoils from every raid we pulled off. We hit merchant ships, weak settlements, and even a few small Imperial outposts. Each time, I got my cut—teef, weapons, and sometimes even food! I won’t lie; it felt good to be rewarded for my strength.

  

I charged into battle with the Orks, my ripper gun roarin' like a beast. We smashed through defenses, and every time we won, I could see the loot pile growin'. The Orks, they love a good fight, and they pay well for muscle like mine. I became part of their crew, and they treated me like one of their own—mostly 'cause I could crush a man with one hand!

  

But I ain't blind, Inquisitor. I know these greenskins are treacherous. They’ll turn on ya if they think they can get more from someone else. But for now, the pay is good, and I’m livin' better than I ever did in the service of the Imperium. I’ve seen things out there, and I know the risks, but I can’t deny the thrill of battle and the promise of wealth.

  

So, what do you say, Inquisitor? I’m just Slabs, an Ogryn lookin' to make a livin' in a galaxy full of war. If the pay's right, I’ll keep swingin’ my fists for whoever’s got the coin. Just remember, I’m not a traitor—I’m a survivor.”

  
  

2c Curriculum Vitae

Provide a detailed account of your previous deployments and operations of the last ten years. Successes and failures alike will be scrutinized.

  

Deployment History:

Operation Wrecking Ball (Year 1):

Objective: Raid a merchant convoy traveling through the Ashen Expanse.

Outcome: Success. Slabs led the charge, utilizing his brute strength to breach the convoy's defenses. The Orks secured a significant haul of supplies and teef, with Slabs receiving a generous share. This operation solidified his reputation among the Ork pirates.

  

Operation Green Tide (Year 2):

Objective: Assist in an assault on a small Imperial outpost.

Outcome: Partial Success. While the Orks overwhelmed the outpost, Slabs sustained injuries during the assault. The Orks celebrated their victory, but Slabs was left with a scar that serves as a reminder of the dangers of underestimating Imperial defenses.

  

Operation Blood Harvest (Year 3):

Objective: Raid a hive city for resources and slaves.

Outcome: Failure. The operation was met with unexpected resistance from local PDF forces. Slabs fought valiantly, but the Orks were forced to retreat, resulting in heavy casualties. Slabs learned the importance of intelligence and preparation in future operations.

  

Operation Loot and Pillage (Year 4):

Objective: Target a rival Ork warband's supply depot.

Outcome: Success. Slabs played a crucial role in the ambush, helping to secure a vast amount of weaponry and supplies. The victory reinforced Slabs' standing among the Orks, and he was rewarded with additional teef and a new ripper gun.

  

Operation Starlight Heist (Year 5):

Objective: Hijack an Imperial transport ship in low orbit.

Outcome: Success. Slabs and the Ork crew executed a daring boarding action, resulting in the capture of valuable cargo. The operation was a turning point, showcasing Slabs' ability to adapt to new environments and tactics.

  

Operation Dark Descent (Year 6):

Objective: Infiltrate a fortified mining facility.

Outcome: Failure. The operation was poorly planned, leading to a disastrous ambush by the facility's security forces. Slabs managed to escape, but the Ork pirates suffered significant losses. This event highlighted the need for better strategic planning.

  

Operation Rampaging Fury (Year 7):

Objective: Conduct a large-scale raid on a planetary capital.

Outcome: Success. Slabs led a brutal assault, resulting in the capture of key political figures and a wealth of resources. The operation was celebrated among the Orks, and Slabs earned the title of "Crusher of the Weak."

  

Operation Warlord's Challenge (Year 8):

Objective: Defend the Ork camp from a retaliatory Imperial strike.

Outcome: Success. Slabs rallied the Ork forces, utilizing his strength to hold the line against the Imperial assault. The defense was a testament to Slabs' leadership and combat prowess.

  

Operation Reckoning (Year 9):

Objective: Retrieve stolen loot from a rival gang.

Outcome: Partial Success. Slabs and the Orks managed to recover some of the stolen goods, but the operation ended in a bloody confrontation, resulting in heavy casualties on both sides. Slabs learned the harsh realities of loyalty among mercenaries.

  

Operation Final Stand (Year 10):

Objective: Assist in a last-ditch effort to secure a valuable resource world.

Outcome: Failure. The Ork forces were overwhelmed by a coordinated Imperial response. Slabs fought bravely but was ultimately captured during the retreat, marking a significant defeat in his career. This operation has left Slabs questioning his future with the Ork pirates and the consequences of his choices.

Recipient: [[Lord Inquisitor Seraphina Valeria]]
Origin: Adeptus Arbites, Hive World Caldris‑8 Enforcement Division  
Security Clearance: PRIORITY ALPHA – HIGH THREAT ANALYSIS  

  
 ### **Subject Profile**  
- Designation: OGR‑271 (Cerebrum Augmented)  
- Classification: Enhanced Ogryn Infantry – Unauthorized xenotech augmentation via Ork neuro‑implants  
- Status: Active; currently under high‑security containment  
- Current Evaluation: Exhibits above‑average intelligence and tactical acumen compared to standard Ogryn units. However, his unsanctioned cranial modifications result in unpredictable decision‑making and potential ideological instability.

 

 **Findings & Threat Assessment**  
• Unorthodox Augmentation: Post‑battlefield recovery operations discovered that OGR‑271’s cerebrum was fitted with invasive Ork implants. These implants, although not of Imperial design, confer a level of strategic insight and rapid cognitive processing remarkably atypical for his species.  
• Enhanced Tactical Capabilities: Field reports attest to his ability to formulate effective albeit unconventional battle strategies—an asset that, if controlled, could offer significant tactical advantages.  
• Behavioral Instability & Ideological Risk: The xenotech source of the implants introduces erratic neural interference. Subject behavior has shown impulsivity and a predisposition toward unorthodox thought patterns. These fluctuations raise serious concerns regarding potential heretical ideation and the subversion of Imperium‑mandated combat doctrine.

• Operational Concerns: Although his enhanced intellect offers a rare tactical edge, the duality of his human (or rather, Ogryn) instincts and augmented cognition may render him a double‑edged sword. His capacity for both brilliant decision‑making and reckless, unfiltered aggression necessitates immediate further study.


 ## Recommended Course of Action  

1. Forensic Re‑Evaluation: Subject’s neural implants must be analyzed and, if possible, recalibrated by Mechanicus tech‑priests experienced with xenos augmentation.  

2. Extended Containment & Surveillance: OGR‑271 is to remain in a high‑security cell fitted with neural dampening fields and under constant observation for any signs of psychological or ideological deviation.  

3. Conditional Tactical Deployment: Should reconditioning prove successful, subject may be considered for experimental deployment in highly controlled engagements, subject to immediate de‑activation on any breach of protocols.  

4. Immediate Neutralization: In the event that his implants prove irredeemably unstable or lead to further heretical behaviors, prompt termination is advised to prevent wider contamination of Imperial forces.

  
 Final Arbites Declaration  

_"OGR‑271 represents a singular anomaly amongst the Ogryn. While his enhanced cognitive abilities might yield unprecedented battlefield advantages, the inherent instability introduced by his unsanctioned Ork implants poses an unacceptable risk to Imperial order. Inquisitorial review is imperative. Should continued operations evidence further instability or ideological deviation, immediate neutralization is recommended."_
