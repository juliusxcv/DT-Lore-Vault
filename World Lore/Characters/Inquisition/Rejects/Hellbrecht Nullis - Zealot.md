## Ordo Xenos Intake

1a Full Name:

Judicar Helbrecht Nullis

  

1b Place of Birth

Hive World

  
  

2a Describe your loyalty to the Emperor

I have burned out the sin from eight heretic congregations across the Helion Reach, sparing none, not even the children, for mercy is not mine to give. 

  

I recited the full Litany of Woe while walking barefoot through the ash of Hive Perdition, where I alone survived the pyre that cleansed its blasphemous clergy.

  

I carried the bones of Saint-Crucian for twenty days across the Dagger Wastes, unarmed and fasting, that they might rest in consecrated soil. 

  

On Duras IV, I exposed and executed a planetary governor for harboring mutants, despite his ties to the Ministorum. 

  

I have not known rest, indulgence, or doubt since my youth—only the Emperor’s will, spoken in flame and silence. 

  

If xenos glyphs mocked me with their presence, I did not read them. I do not need alien words to recognize impurity. My devotion is proven not in what I say, but in what I have burned.

  
  

2b I have come into contact with the following factions:

  

Drukhari

Genestealer Cults

Necrons

  
  

2b Incident Documentation:

While purging a warp-corrupted cult on Vardis Prime, I encountered a small Eldar scouting party near a desecrated shrine. I recognized their glyphs as ancient, not born of ruinous powers, and chose restraint—not from sympathy, but because divine insight told me this was a test of faith. My actions were driven by duty: to discern true corruption from illusion, to avoid hastening damnation by killing where repentance was possible. The Eldar withdrew, their motives unclear but their presence a warning.

  

Later, on Syrax IV, I faced a brief encounter with Tau diplomats seeking parley. Though the Imperium forbids dealings with xenos, I saw their approach as a trial of the Emperor’s light, not an alliance. My sermons burned hotter afterward, and I led a swift purge of those Tau sympathizers who dared spread alien influence.

  

My contact with these races was never from survival or impulse but born of unwavering duty—always to protect the Imperium’s soul. The outcomes were clear: heresy and xenos taint were rooted out, and the Emperor’s justice delivered without hesitation. There is no ambiguity in my loyalty, only relentless faith.

  
  

2c Curriculum Vitae

Born in the hive-depths of Virellia Prime, Helbrecht Nullis was orphaned young and raised by the Ecclesiarchy in the Schola Orphanum, where his unshakable faith and harsh intolerance set him apart. He rose swiftly through the ranks, trained not as a comforting preacher but as a weapon of doctrine. Chosen for the path of the Judicar, he became a roving enforcer of Imperial faith, feared and revered in equal measure. His sermons were fire and iron, his presence a promise of judgment—and wherever he walked, heresy burned.

  

Judicar Helbrecht Nullis is a sanctioned agent of the Ecclesiarchy, a tireless enforcer of faith who roams the Imperium delivering judgment to the heretic and unworthy. Cloaked in soot-stained robes and bearing the Writ of Flame, he speaks often in scripture, his voice a relentless sermon of fire and fury. To many, he is a saint in the making; to others, a fanatic too consumed by his own righteousness—but all agree his faith burns without end.

  

He has faced the insidious whispers of the Genestealer Cults, rooting them out from beneath the spires of forgotten hives, watching their twisted rituals burn beneath his prayers. The cruel cruelty of the Drukhari he has met in the shadows of shattered worlds, their venomous blades unable to pierce the shield of his faith. Against the cold, unyielding legions of the Necrons, he has stood firm, knowing that steel and death are no match for the Emperor’s eternal light. The xenos Eldar once sought parley, their silver tongues weaving lies—he rejected their poison outright, seeing only the decay beneath their veils. Each encounter has been a test of his soul, and each time the flame of the Emperor’s judgment has purified the darkness.

  

Years ago, while cleansing a remote agri-world of warp-tainted cultists, Judicar Helbrecht Nullis was accused by a rival priest of consorting with xenos after he spared a village that bore strange glyphs on its shrine stones. The glyphs, later suspected to be of alien origin, cast doubt on his judgment and loyalty. Though he claimed divine intuition guided his mercy, the Ordo Hereticus intervened, seizing him for interrogation and holding him in solitary confinement within a sealed bastion beneath the Schola Progenium. There he remains to this day, stripped of rank but unrepentant—chanting litanies through bloodied lips, convinced the Emperor will vindicate him in fire or in death.

I was raised in silence. No name. No kin. Just scripture… and steel.  

The Schola Orphanum does not teach kindness. It teaches obedience. I learned the Litany of Fire before I learned my own age.

 When the others slept, I prayed. When they faltered, I judged.

On the eighth purge of the Helion Reach, I burned eight congregations to cinder. Sparing none. ...Not even their children.  

They say I should have wept. I recited the full Litany of Woe instead. Every word and every breath.  

Faith does not wear masks. I exposed a planetary governor who cloaked mutants in faith. The Ecclesiarchy hesitated. I did not. 

  

I carried the remains of Saint-Crucian through twenty days of barren ruin. Fasting. Unarmed. My feet split. My faith did not.

On Syrax IV, the Tau came. Voices smooth as oil, bearing peace behind alien teeth. I listened. Not to yield. But to locate those who would. And when I found them. I burned them. My sermons carried twice the heat that week.  

I stood before the Necrons. I thought I knew evil—warpspawn, heretic flame, xenos seduction. But this was colder. No chants. No fury.

  

They did not bleed. They did not burn. And for the first time in service… I knew fear. And somehow… the Emperor let me run.

 That day, I did not triumph. And now I know what death truly looks like. Cold. Ancient. Patient.  

And when the glyphs came, alien and ancient, I withheld the blade. Not for sympathy. But because the fire whispered, "Not yet."  

So they buried me. Said I consorted with the xenos. Said mercy makes traitors and hesitation is heresy. In flame, I found clarity. And in silence, devotion. Yet  judgment still unfinished.  

I walk again. Not for forgiveness. Not for glory. But because fire must finish what mercy cannot.

The Emperor does not ask for kindness. And I... I burn in His name.



**SUBJECT PROFILE**

Recipient: [[Lord Inquisitor Seraphina Valeria]]
**DESIGNATION**: Judicar Helbrecht Nullis
**CLASSIFICATION**: Sanctioned Ecclesiarchal Enforcer – Exiled Status
**STATUS**: In solitary confinement beneath Bastion-Crypt Durex Sanctum; sermonizing under Null-Field restraint

**EVALUATION**
Once among the most effective roving Judicars of the Helion Reach, subject was stripped of rank after exhibiting unauthorized discretionary mercy and doctrinal deviance. Faith remains unbroken. Fire remains active.

**Zealous Record of Purge**
Helbrecht Nullis executed eight full-scale burns across hive shrines in the Helion Reach, including Hive Perdition and Duras IV. High casualty rates included sanctioned clergy, unregistered mutants, and civilian dependents. Subject consistently refused to distinguish by age, claiming “the Emperor’s judgment does not weep.”

**Deviation through Discernment**
Notable restraint displayed during xenos-adjacent incidents. On Vardis Prime, Nullis spared a shrine marked by ancient Eldar glyphs. On Syrax IV, he observed Tau diplomatic envoys without immediate purge, instead executing known sympathizers post-interaction. The Ecclesiarchy ruled such restraint heretical; Nullis insists “discernment is a blade, not a shield.**

**Necron Encounter – Severe Psychological Impact**
Survived direct contact with awakened Necron units during an unsanctioned relic retrieval. Witness testimonies describe Nullis retreating under prayer, wounded and incoherent. Post-event writings equate Necrons to “the absence of soul, the Emperor's inverse, death that thinks.” Subject's recollection displays trauma yet intensified devotion.

**Interrogation Outcome**
Detained following accusations of xenos complicity and unsanctioned mercy. Subject offered neither denial nor justification—only continuous recitation of penitential litanies. Despite prolonged confinement, spiritual decay has not been observed. Faith remains fortified.

**Recommended Ecclesiarchal Actions  
1. **Continued Null-Field Confinement:** Maintain isolation and suppression protocols. Subject’s presence incites doctrinal unrest among adjacent clergy. Weekly recitation logs to be reviewed for heretical phrasing.  ‍
2. **Limited Contact Authorization:** Inquisitorial visitation permitted under strict sigil-cleared escort. Subject remains cooperative, but his rhetorical charisma may sway the uncertain.  ‍
3. **Doctrinal Reconciliation Consideration:** Though exiled, subject’s operational record is undeniably effective. Reinstatement under probationary Inquisitorial command may yield decisive results in fringe operations where standard purity cannot survive.  ‍
4. **Containment Termination (Conditional):** Should subject display ideological escalation, uncontrolled psychic resonance (documented following Necron contact), or attempt escape, full dissolution rites are authorized.  ‍

**Final Arbites Declaration**
Helbrecht Nullis is both blade and burn mark—carved deep into the parchment of Imperial doctrine. He is no longer useful to the Ecclesiarchy, for he sees too much and spares too selectively. But to the Inquisition… where fire must walk among shadow… he may be precisely what survives.
