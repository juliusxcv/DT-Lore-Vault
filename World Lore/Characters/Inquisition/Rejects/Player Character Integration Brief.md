# Player Character Integration Brief

Compressed, injection-ready reference so worldbuilding stays character-aware. This is the companion to the worldbuilding system prompt: the prompt governs *behaviour*, this supplies the player-character *context* its canon hierarchy defers to. Upload alongside the [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Rejects|Rejects]] files to project knowledge for cross-device sessions. Full backstories live in the individual files; this is the operational digest.

## How to use this
- Consult the **Hook Matrix** before inventing an NPC, location, or faction — a player character may already reach into it.
- Character-personal reveals obey a per-character **reveal-tier ceiling** (Texture → Anomaly → Name). Never exceed a character's ceiling for the current beat.
- Any content touching a PC's backstory element gets flagged to the GM before it reaches player-facing material.

## The Knowledge Ladder — who knows what about the Shard

The engine of the campaign's dramatic irony. Preserve the gaps; do not let a higher tier's knowledge leak downward.

| Party | What they actually know | What they think it is |
|---|---|---|
| [[World Lore/Characters/Dark Adeptus Mechanicus/The Body\|Dark Mechanicus]] | The true nature; working to free it and complete its ascension | A nascent god to be born |
| [[World Lore/Characters/Drukhari/Shards of Venom\|Shards of Venom]] | Nothing of its origin; want it for arena-beasts and prestige | Exotic gene-splicing xenotech |
| [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Ordo Xenos/Lord Inquisitor Seraphina Valeria\|Lord Inquisitor Valeria]] | **Nothing of the Shard.** Hyperfocused on finding [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Ordo Xenos/Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan\|Harridan]]; blindsided to the warning signs | "The matter Harridan chased" — a trail, not a truth |
| Isabella Alderidge | That she dealt Drukhari, breached a relic, and disaster followed | A gene-accelerator she failed to control |
| The Rejects (players) | Fragments, phenomena, dead ends | Unknown |

**Structural note:** Valeria's ignorance is load-bearing. It is *why* she deploys expendable operatives onto an anchor-Shard without flinching. Never write her as quietly knowing more than this — her blindness is the deployment's justification.

## Squad Layer

**Relationship web**
- **Nullis ↔ Thornkell** — open hostility; Nullis formally requested sanction to execute her; **denied**, Nullis under standing stand-down order. He is, without realising it, a second observer of her escalation.
- **Nullis ↔ Alderidge** — genuine respect; he commends her courage unprompted.
- **Thornkell → ALB-XXIII** — thawing; "a certain elegance to his demeanour."
- **Rook** — orbits the squad; repeatedly drops out of others' observation. Slabs notices.
- **Slabs** — blunt loyalty to "Boss Lady"; reads the room more than his register suggests.

**Valeria's leverage, per operative** — every Reject was in custody, under threat assessment, or under existential leverage. She owns them individually: Rook (unresolved compromise verdict), Nullis (Vardis Prime, the mercy that damned him), Thornkell (spared reconditioning/termination), Slabs (captured asset), ALB (renegade deferred to her), Alderidge (the erasable reactor-failure record).

**Who reports** — Nullis, Thornkell, Slabs file mission reports; Vex's are the richest and the most self-incriminating. **Rook and Alderidge file nothing** — their silences are themselves characterisation.

## Per-Character Integration

### Vexilia Thornkell — Psyker
- **Kernel:** unsanctioned psyker; everything routes through "the Mask."
- **Levers:** the mirrored-mask performer and the half-laughing/half-crying visage (Harlequin-adjacent imagery — *never confirm*); rift-sensitivity; Ichar IV / Tyranid / Genestealer past.
- **Threads & state:** escalating manifestations — bilocation and a temporal slip on Mission III; retains mutated bio-specimens against Valeria's containment order; self-appointed "unraveller" of the phenomena, on a collision course with the observe-first directive; has already keyed the Mask ↔ [[World Lore/Beastiary/Fauna/Hollowkin|Hollowkin]] / Silent Watchers link.
- **Player knows / GM-only:** she knows her own experiences; the Mask's true nature is GM-only and *deliberately unresolved* — the veteran-facing rift-signature clue (no warp signature) is hers to carry.
- **Reveal ceiling:** Anomaly. She is the party's living Shard-detector — her rift-sensitivity is the diagnostic instrument for the moon's non-warp cosmology. What the Mask *is* = Name-tier, reserved for the endgame.

### Isabella Alderidge — Rogue Trader
- **Kernel:** Rogue Trader by design; serves under Valeria's leverage after the Crystal Covenant.
- **Levers:** the [[World Lore/Characters/Drukhari/Shards of Venom|Shards of Venom]] and Archon [[World Lore/Characters/Drukhari/Operatives/Archon Vaelinth Sissarath|Vaelinth Sissarath]] (the same predator who used her fleet — now on Tenebris); Lucien the arco-flagellant; xenos-trade fluency.
- **Threads & state:** her isolated fragment rides aboard the *Imperialis Aegis* as a mid-to-endgame escalation device; the Shards of Venom are a *present* faction, so the recognition hook is staged, not hypothetical; Lucien's whereabouts open.
- **Player knows / GM-only:** she believes her artifact was a gene-accelerator; that it was a lesser Shard of the same fractured entity beneath Tenebris is GM-only, as is Valeria's true (shard-trace) motive.
- **Reveal ceiling:** Texture/Anomaly. Her fragment's kinship to the Tenebris Shard = Name-tier, reserved for the Dark Citadel vicinity. She must not connect her past to this moon early.

### Gideon Rook — Veteran
- **Kernel:** Tempestus Aquilon sole survivor; six unaccounted hours aboard a space hulk; does not himself know if he is compromised.
- **Levers:** **the hulk** — pull this thread at the [[World Lore/Places/Luna Tenebris/Midnight Wraith|Midnight Wraith]] threshold; Penitent's Vigil guilt; the buried questions.
- **Threads & state:** compromise unproven and unprovable; recurring observation-gaps on Tenebris (Scion fieldcraft, or something else); files no reports.
- **Player knows / GM-only:** his record and the hulk incident are known; whether he is *actually* compromised is a **GM dial (open)** — as is the identity of his augmetic-eyed interrogator (may or may not have been Valeria).
- **Reveal ceiling:** Anomaly. The hulk-descent is his Name-tier payoff — the campaign's endgame walks him back into the exact scenario that broke him.

### Slabs — Ogryn
- **Kernel:** Ogryn heavy with **genuine Ork Painboy cranial implants** (installed under Warboss Grommuk); blunt cunning that outstrips his register.
- **Levers:** xenos implants on a Dark-Symmetry moon; Ork-merc past.
- **Threads & state:** implant stability/drift is an open question; percussive cogitator "persuasion" now sanctioned *only* on Valeria's explicit order; notices what others miss (Rook's disappearances).
- **Player knows / GM-only:** everyone knows he has a metal head; whether those implants make him a *receiver* — or a vulnerability — for the Shard's machine-and-mind influence is a **GM dial (open)**.
- **Reveal ceiling:** Texture. His implants as an unsettling background detail before any Anomaly attaches to them.

### ALB-XXIII — Skitarii
- **Kernel:** rogue, self-aware Skitarii Vanguard; hunted by the orthodox Mechanicus; servo-skull B-III at his side.
- **Levers:** the [[World Lore/Characters/Dark Adeptus Mechanicus/The Body|Dark Mechanicus]] — the one branch of his creed that might *welcome* his sentience; unusual machine-spirit rapport; his quest for lost humanity.
- **Threads & state:** Mechanicus hunt ongoing; a Dark-AdMech temptation/recruitment arc the antagonists would be foolish not to attempt; his rapport near *corrupted* machinery is an asset — or a backdoor.
- **Player knows / GM-only:** he is a known renegade Skitarii; whether the Dark Mechanicus court him, and whether his rapport extends to Shard-corrupted systems, are **GM dials (open)**.
- **Reveal ceiling:** Anomaly.

### Hellbrecht Nullis — Zealot
- **Kernel:** exiled Ecclesiarchal Judicar; his one act of *mercy* is the sin that damned him; carries deep Necron trauma ("death that thinks").
- **Levers:** the undead moon presses the Necron bruise relentlessly; Vardis Prime is Valeria's leash; his zealotry vs. squad cohesion.
- **Threads & state:** standing stand-down order re Thornkell; necrotic/thinking-dead encounters are personal triggers; second observer of Vex's escalation.
- **Player knows / GM-only:** his record and exile are known; how far the leash holds, and whether Tenebris's thinking-death breaks or hardens him, are for play to determine.
- **Reveal ceiling:** Texture/Anomaly.

## Hook Matrix — loaded intersections

Where a player character reaches into a campaign pillar. Use these before inventing adjacent content.

- **Shard / [[World Lore/Places/Luna Tenebris/Midnight Wraith|Midnight Wraith]]** — *Rook* (the descent payoff); *Isabella* (the *Aegis* fragment, resonance/escalation); *Vex* (rift-sensitivity = detector).
- **[[World Lore/Characters/Dark Adeptus Mechanicus/The Body|Dark Mechanicus]]** — *ALB-XXIII* (temptation/recruitment); *Slabs* (implant vulnerability); the Shard's machine-corruption vector touches both.
- **[[World Lore/Characters/Drukhari/Shards of Venom|Shards of Venom]]** — *Isabella* (direct: former counterparties, present threat, Sissarath recognition). The single most personal faction-hook on the board.
- **[[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Ordo Xenos/Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan|Harridan]]** — every Reject indirectly (Valeria's hunt for him is why they are all here); the Bolt Pistol breadcrumb sits in Koln's footlocker, not a PC file.
- **[[World Lore/Characters/Miners/The Green Angel|The Green Angel]] / the Fallen** — *Vex* (may perceive/name the anomaly before others); tier-capped — the Fallen must never name the Dark Soul pre-Citadel.
- **[[World Lore/Beastiary/Fauna/Hollowkin|Hollowkin]] / Silent Watchers** — *Vex* (already wired via the Mask); *Isabella* (proposed Eldar, from her xenos-trade lens).
- **[[World Lore/Places/Luna Tenebris/Project HELIOS SHEPHERD|HELIOS / SHEPHERD]]** — the Titan boss fight precedes the descent; the [[World Lore/Places/Luna Tenebris/Research Facility/0_Theta Gate/Corvus Blackstar|Corvus Blackstar]] on the Landing Platform and the experimental Corvus tank are the player assets staged against it.
- **[[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Deathwatch/Killteam Velatum Umbra|Velatum Umbra]]** — the Shards of Venom are *pursuing the killteam*; endgame convergence, where Isabella's Drukhari and the friendly-NPC survivors collide beneath the moon.

**Convergence to protect:** two independent hunters (Valeria's Inquisition and the Shards of Venom) and one lost killteam all draw toward the same Shard by different roads. Isabella is the seam where the Inquisition strand and the Drukhari strand were *already* knotted once. Let that tighten slowly.

## Standing Integration Rules
1. Check the Hook Matrix before inventing NPCs/locations/factions; a PC may already reach the space.
2. Respect each character's reveal-tier ceiling; personal arcs pace against the Dark Soul arc, not ahead of it.
3. Flag any content touching a PC's backstory element to the GM before it appears in player-facing material.
4. Preserve the Knowledge Ladder — no downward leaks, Valeria's ignorance especially.
5. Mark anything not already ratified `[NEW CANON]`; open dials stay labelled as dials, not asserted as fact.
