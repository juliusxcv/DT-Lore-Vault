# The Rejects

Operative cell assembled and run by Lord Inquisitor Seraphina Valeria, Ordo Xenos. The unifying trait of the roster: every member was in Inquisitorial custody, under active threat assessment, or under existential leverage when Valeria claimed them. Compromised assets no other authority would touch — who therefore answer to her alone.

Currently deployed on Luna Tenebris (998.M41). Reporting via secure vox relay to the *Imperialis Aegis*.

## Roster

- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/ALB-XXIII - Skitari|ALB-XXIII]] — rogue, self-aware Skitarii Vanguard; flagged renegade, hunted by the Mechanicus
- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Gideon Rook - Veteran|Gideon Rook]] — Tempestus Aquilon sole survivor; suspected compromise, investigation never concluded
- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Hellbrecht Nullis - Zealot|Hellbrecht Nullis]] — exiled Ecclesiarchal Judicar; doctrinal deviance, pulled from Null-Field confinement
- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Isabella Alderidge - Rogue Trader|Isabella Alderidge]] — Rogue Trader; spared after the Crystal Covenant, serves under Valeria's leverage
- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Slabs - Ogryn|Slabs]] — Ogryn ex-mercenary of Ork pirates; genuine Painboy cranial implants, captured asset
- [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Vexilia Thornkell - Psyker|Vexilia Thornkell]] — unsanctioned psyker; escalating warp phenomena tied to "the Mask"
