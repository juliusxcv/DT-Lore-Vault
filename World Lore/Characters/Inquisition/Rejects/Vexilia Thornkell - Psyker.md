Vexilia Thornkell, but people call me Vex

2c Curriculum Vitae

Provide a detailed account of your previous deployments and operations of the last ten years. Successes and failures alike will be scrutinized.

  

“I was born in the lower levels of the hive world Ichar IV, prior to the second tyrannic war, to a father who worked as a Mag-Lift operator and a mother who was a Manufactorum worker, attaching wires to hotshot packs day in day out. 

  

I was a difficult child. My nightmares that started in my early childhood, shortly after my parents took me to see a play of a travelling troupe of performers… I still remember like it was yesterday that one of these slender, tall performers, their face hidden behind a mirrored mask, approached me after their play, gently touched my forehead. After that, these nightmares began, visions of impossible places, terrible creatures and a recurring Mask, a grimace, maybe even a face - half laughing, half crying… however strange, I am now convinced this Mask must be a symbol of the Emperor's will. Strange things happened whenever I was upset, and I was upset a lot: lights flickering, objects falling without reason, cogitators and auto-quills erratically spewing nonsense… people were always feeling uneasy around me. Only when I was 12 years old, it became clear that this was my psychic powers were manifesting. In a fit of rage over a stolen toy I sent a girl from prayer group flying across the room. My parents didn’t handle it well, knowing the ministorum would soon be knocking on our door. The very next day, father took me to the mag-lifts he operated and ditched me down an open shaft. I let it happen. Possibly because the night before, I dreamt of the Mask again, and it told me in an eerie, melodic language I had never heard before, that I should.

  

I landed in the dumps of the underhive, incredibly lucky to have survived the drop at all. Well, maybe luck didn't have much to do with it. In any case, I found myself lost in the dark of the underhive, sobbing, wandering around aimlessly until I found myself surrounded by terrifying mutants wearing rags of human skin. They were already clawing at my clothes, scratching my skin, and if it hadn't been for the Mask, I would surely be dead - for at the last moment, the visage burned itself in my mind, and when I came to, all of them were dead. There was no blood, only stiff corpses, limbs twisted and dislocated, with insane smiles on their disfigured faces.

  

After that, I barely managed to survive on my own for quite some time, staying hidden as well as I could, drinking dirty water, eating whatever scraps or vermin I managed to get my hands on, only using my powers in the most grave of situations. I was maybe 13 or 14, and severely malnourished when I was met with the first act of kindness in the underhive. ‘Stitch-eye’ Marven, a strange, mutated man claiming to belong to some ‘Brotherhood’ offered me some food… I knew from prayer group that you should never trust the mutant, but I was desperate. I followed him to a kind of conclave and stayed there for some time, the man always insinuating how valuaɓle a healthy human like myself would be, preaching that I would play a crucial part in Ichar IV's salvation, that I am so lucky that I would see the light of the god-head, the cosmic embrace, the voice in the void. I thought these to be strange names for our one and only God-Emperor until I was to receive the god-head's kiss. I even heard the voice in the void whisper to me as the mutant lead me before an abominable, terrifying creature with needle-like teeth, four arms with razor-sharp claws and glowing eyes that seemed to pierce my very soul… and it seemed very displeased with it, because it ripped the mutant entwine before going after me. My instincts saved me - or maybe it was more, for I once again saw that fateful Mask in my mind's eye - but I managed to channel all my psychic might into a stream of lightning that burned the creature's skin and muscle away alike. I ran from that sacral chamber as fast and far as I could, once again lost and alone in the underhive.

  

That was when Eska Varro found me. She was the first being I ever felt a true connection to, and I felt it right from the start. Even the Mask seemed to be pleased with her. She was a member of the Gutter Saints gang, the most graceful and delicate woman I had ever seen… and an unsanctioned psyker, just like me! In a way, she reminded me of the slender figure who had touched my forehead so many years ago. She took me under her wing, taught me to control and use my abilities, she taught me about the Brotherhood’s insanity, about the God-Emperor's big plans for me. The Gutter Saints were like the family I had never had. I spent years with Eska and the other Gutter Saints: Marda Vox, the former Sister of Battle, who taught me all about our glorious Emperor and how suffering for Him is the greatest honour; Brother Grell, who taught me when it's better to stay silent; Sister Marr, who taught me how to tend to the wounds from speaking up in the wrong moments; Hessa Crend, who taught me both to drink and how to handle myself in a fistfight. How many chaos worshipping dirtbags we smoked out, how many gatherings of the Brotherhood we busted… Oh, I miss them all dearly. But I miss Eska most of them all.

  

I figure you don't need me to tell you what happened to them? Turns out the cosmic embrace was real all along… a terrible, horrifying embrace of a million creatures, endless teeth and endless claws, the Great Devourer. The voice in the void slowly grew louder over the years, from the faintest whisper to a deafening clarion. The Brotherhood revelled when the tyranid swarm arrived, but me and The Gutter Saints? We fought. We fought with everything we had. One by one, the Gutter Saints were torn apart until only I was left. When I saw Eska disappear in the clawed masses, I yearned to die with her, but the Mask wouldn't let me. Whenever I was close, it burned itself into my mind and, by some miracle, I was saved by yet another tear in reality, yet another platoon of the 13th penal legion turning up, yet another lucky collapse of city rubble. In the aftermath of the invasion I searched for any sign of Eska, dead or alive, until your people scooped me up. The history books may credit Marneus Calgar with the victory on my home world, but without people like us, Ichar IV would have fallen long before their arrival.

  

And well, I suppose that brings us here.”

## Recruitment & Current Status
Released from high-risk containment following Inquisitorial review by Lord Inquisitor Seraphina Valeria, Ordo Xenos, and inducted into [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Rejects|the Rejects]]. Currently deployed on Luna Tenebris (998.M41), reporting to Valeria aboard the *Imperialis Aegis*.

**Standing directives from Valeria (Mission II):**
- Recovered biological specimens remain contained; no psychic communion, ritual consultation, or independent experimentation involving them or "the Mask."
- Document all phenomena precisely: direction of perceived origin, emotional state preceding manifestation, accompanying psychic/environmental disturbances, correlation with hostile activity or anomalous structures.
- Observe first, indulge instinct second.

## Campaign Development Log
*(Compiled from operative mission reports. Update each cycle.)*
- **Mission I:** Warp lightning cleared the risen dead in the settlement — witnessed by Nullis, whose hostility briefly wavered ("a vessel of Your storm").
- **Mission II:** Injured ("scratched"). Recovered strangely mutated biological specimens and retained them. First reports of the "beautiful, frightening music" from the forest. Found the Helbrun field report and echoed its claim that "the moon is singing" — while suspecting the singer is not the moon itself. Witnessed the mining-shaft displacement of Slabs and Rook. Her passing reference to "the Valeth girl" speaking of the shadows = Mila Valev, of the settlement's [[World Lore/Characters/Miners/The Valev Family|Valev Family]] (name garbled by Vex).
- **Mission III — escalation event:** Bilocation — "became two for a little while," described as the Mask's flesh merging back into her; followed by a momentary slip in her "place in time." Took ("stole") a protective item while destabilized; only Nullis appeared to notice. Heard, through "the eyes and ears of the Mask": *"We called them the Hollow Kin, because they never spoke and never stayed."* With Slabs' help, extracted the Silent Watchers record from the cogitators. Explicitly connects the Mask ↔ the childhood play ↔ the Silent Watchers' description ("Tall. Slender. Movement like smoke."). Alderidge proposed Eldar; Vex wants to see them.
- **Watch item (GM):** She has appointed herself the one who will "unravel" the phenomena — direct collision course with Valeria's observe-first directive.
