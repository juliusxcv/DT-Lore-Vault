# Campaign Chronology

The canonical timeline (the spine) for the Doomtroopers campaign. Every date below is ratified. When a note's internal date conflicts with this spine, the spine wins — flag and fix the note.

**Reading the dates.** Format is inherited from the source documents and is mixed: `M40.xxx` is the handout's millennium-fraction style (late in the 40th millennium); `731.M41` / `995.M41` is standard year-of-millennium. Both are correct as written; don't "normalise" them without a deliberate pass.

## GM Notes

- **Anchors kept:** mining era M38.992–M40.229 (handout), facility reoccupation 731.M41, present 998.M41.
- **Assigned this rework:** the M40.218 / .220 / .221 materialisation cluster; the 995–997.M41 collapse cluster; the [[Killteam Velatum Umbra]] loss moved 869.M41 → 997.M41 so the recent flare-up (stranded Sentinels, the killteam) is actually recent.
- **Harridan's longevity — ratified.** ~1,700 years between his seal-break (M40.218) and his return (995.M41), sustained by heretical means. Fits his radical caste; possibly the Wraith's own influence reaching back along the hunt.
- **Crystal Covenant — added this cycle.** Tributary entry `[NEW CANON]`; date deliberately soft (~990.M41, within 5 years of the 995 flare) — pin precisely only if a later beat demands it. Establishes Valeria's Tenebris vector and her acquisition of [[World Lore/Characters/Inquisition/Rejects/Isabella Alderidge - Rogue Trader|Isabella Alderidge]]; does **not** alter the spine.
- **Still open:** the three-layer seal model in [[Dark Citadel]] is flagged `[NEW CANON]` — dates are locked, the "broken-once vs. partially-intact" reconciliation is not yet formally ratified.
- **Do not "fix" the handout.** `Tenebris - Historical Journal` keeps the false abandonment cause on purpose — it's in-world misdirection. The truth lives here and in the GM notes of the Wraith/Shard/Citadel.
