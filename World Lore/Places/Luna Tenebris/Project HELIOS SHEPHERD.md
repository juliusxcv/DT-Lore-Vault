## Project HELIOS / SHEPHERD

Classified Adeptus Biologis–Mechanicus weapons program conducted in the lowest research levels of [[Research Facility Tenebris]]. Two paired experimental **Warhound Scout Titans**, developed as a single weapon system split across two chassis. Neither functions as a war engine alone; each is built around the other's flaw.

The program is the direct cause of the facility's downfall. Its final weapons test breached the strata beneath the test range and opened an uncontained wound into the [[Midnight Wraith]], the space hulk anchored below — triggering the Inquisitorial response that brought [[Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan]] and, in time, the corruption now overrunning the deep levels.

---

## The Two Engines

### HELIOS — the Gun
A Warhound stripped of nearly everything non-essential to feed a single primary weapon far beyond Scout-class tolerance. Armour, secondaries and redundancy were all sacrificed to the mount and its reactor draw. The cost is a brutal vent-and-cooldown cycle after each full discharge: for that window HELIOS is near-defenceless — reactor exposed, machine effectively blind and naked. Left on its own it is a glass cannon that dies in its own recovery phase.

### SHEPHERD — the Shield
The answer to that window. Voidship-grade shield generators crammed into a Warhound chassis, throwing a single envelope **large enough to cover both Titans at once**. It carries almost no offensive capability — it is a walking aegis. The shield is enormously power-hungry and **cannot be sustained continuously**; raising it draws everything the pair can spare.

## The Combat Cycle — Shoot → Shield → Shoot

Power can serve the gun or the bubble, never both. This gives the pair a hard, readable rhythm:

- **SHOOTING** — HELIOS fires. The shield is **down**. Both Titans are exposed. Catastrophic output, but naked.
- **SHIELDING** — the envelope is **up**, covering both hulls. The gun is **cold**, venting. Invulnerable, but toothless.

The pair alternates between the two states. They are almost never both firing *and* shielded. The seam between phases — the transition, and the SHOOTING window — is the only place they can be hurt.

**Encounter role.** The corrupted pair are the **guardians of the descent**, standing over the very breach they tore open. The fight is a puzzle, not an arithmetic contest: players cannot out-gun either Titan through the shield, so the win condition is to **break the co-dependency** — knock the two out of sync, or neutralise one role so the other is left useless (a shield with no gun, or a gun with no cover). Detailed phase/asset design to follow as a separate encounter note.

## The Breach Event

Project HELIOS/SHEPHERD ran in the facility's lowest levels for years under deep compartmentalisation. The research division was **not cleared** to know what lay beneath their firing range — the [[Midnight Wraith]] had materialised and anchored below Luna Tenebris in M40.220, over a thousand years before Tenebris was reoccupied and expanded, and had lain dark and undetected ever since.

During a live-fire test in **995.M41**, HELIOS's overpowered discharge collapsed the range floor and exposed a shallow protrusion of the hulk's mass. This was not the discovery of the Wraith's existence (known at high levels), but the opening of an **uncontained wound** connecting the facility interior to the hulk. A space hulk breaching into a secret Mechanicus base was a catastrophic security event.

The breach tripped the alert that reached the Ordo Xenos. [[Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan]] — already hunting the Wraith for over a thousand years — responded, seized the facility, and began the shard experiments that unleashed active corruption upward through the deep levels.

> **Materialisation mechanism (core secret).** The Wraith did not crash and was not stranded by outside force. The hulk *wants* the warp; the **Shard of the Dark Soul** aboard it wants to remain in real matter space. When the hulk tried to complete its rift-jump, the Shard clawed itself into the moon and anchored, halting the jump short. The Wraith is a body at war with itself — an engine straining to leave, a passenger refusing to go. See [[Shard]], [[Midnight Wraith]].

## Corruption & Current State

When the breach opened, the two minds most powerfully wired into the facility's most powerful machines — the Princeps of HELIOS and SHEPHERD, manifold-bonded and sitting closest to an unfiltered shard-wound — were the first and most completely taken. They did not malfunction; they were **claimed**. The corrupted pair now stand sentinel over the breach, guarding the way down.

[[Killteam Velatum Umbra]] **temporarily disabled** the pair during their own descent. The Titans the players inherit are re-activated and fully hostile — what "disabled" left behind (damage state, residual systems) is open for encounter design.

*Split-audience note.* Mutant Chronicles veterans will read the corruption as the Dark Symmetry — the machines as the Shard's first instruments, the Princeps as the equivalent of Apostle-touched. 40K newcomers will read a Dark Mechanicus daemon-engine guardian. Both readings run on the same content; no reconciliation needed at the table.

## Thread — The Fallen

The Dark Angels connection runs through here. The Deathwing expedition that first breached the Citadel seal came *for* [[The Green Angel]] — he was their quarry, not their ally. When the Wraith woke and the expedition extracted, the Fallen was left aboard and rode the hulk down into Luna Tenebris, trapped ever since. He is the one witness present at the original breach, the jump, and the anchoring — which is what makes him the confession-source deep in the campaign. Later, Interrogator-Chaplain Kael of [[Killteam Velatum Umbra]] broke formation and died pursuing that same Fallen: a multi-generational First Legion failure the Dark Angels can never speak of.
