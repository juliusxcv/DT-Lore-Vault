## **Inquisitorial Report – Artifact Designate: “Shard of the Dark Soul”**

**Document Code:** IX-XN/DS-998.77-HK **Classification:** Omega-Level Containment – Eyes Only **Filed By:** Inquisitor Harridan, Ordo Xenos **Status:** Active – Quarantined

### I. **Summary of Incident**

In **M40.220**, the space hulk _[[Midnight Wraith]]_ materialised within the crust of _Luna Tenebris_, breaching standard warp emergence protocols and bypassing all known detection grids. Anchored deep below the crater basin near Research Station Tenebris, it carried within it a massive citadel-like structure of unknown origin—designated **Citadel Obscura**. The hulk then fell dark, emitting no warp signature, and lay undetected for over a thousand years.

It was relocated in **995.M41**, after the Helios weapons test breached the strata above it. Inquisitorial scans revealed anomalous energy signatures, non-Euclidean architecture, and psychic interference consistent with **xeno-archaeotech corruption**. Within the citadel’s core, a sealed chamber was located containing a crystalline mass—later identified as a **Shard of the Dark Soul**, a transdimensional entity of unknown origin.

### II. **Artifact Profile: Shard of the Dark Soul**

- **Origin:** Extra-universal; not native to known warp-space or material reality
- **Composition:** Non-reactive crystal matrix; emits low-frequency psychic resonance
- **Behavior:** Passive containment; active corruption of surrounding matter and thought
- **Capabilities:**
    - Genetic manipulation of dead tissue
    - Psychic influence over susceptible minds
    - Rift-space distortion (non-warp FTL anomalies)
    - Long-range telepathic projection
    - Indirect control over mechanical systems (Dark Mechanicus infiltration suspected)

### III. **Historical Context (Redacted)**

Recovered fragments from pre-Imperial archives and forbidden texts suggest the **Dark Soul** is a primordial force fractured by the emergence of Light. The entity exists in cyclical phases of dormancy and devastation, deploying **Apostles**—semi-autonomous extensions of its will—to corrupt and consume sentient life.

The current shard is believed to be a remnant of the Dark Soul’s last defeat, sealed by an entity referred to as the **Ancient Enemy**, during a conflict predating the Great Crusade. The seal remains partially intact, though the shard continues to exert influence.

### IV. **Inquisitorial Involvement**

Following the seizure of Tenebris in **995.M41**, Inquisitor Harridan authorized covert experimentation on the shard, seeking to:

- Reanimate dead tissue for battlefield deployment
- Unlock **Rift-Travel**, a theoretical alternative to warp-based FTL
- Harness the shard’s mutagenic properties for controlled evolution

All experiments were terminated in **997.M41** following mass psychogenic collapse of research personnel and the spontaneous activation of dormant systems within _Midnight Wraith_. Harridan has since been declared **missing in action**.

### VI. **Containment Protocols**

- **Citadel Obscura** sealed under Protocol Umbra-Null
- All access restricted to Omega-clearance operatives
- Vox silence enforced across Luna Tenebris
- No attempts to extract or destroy the shard authorized

### VII. **Final Annotation – Ordo Xenos**

> “The shard is not a weapon. It is a whisper. A memory. A hunger. It does not seek escape. It seeks _audience_. And it has begun to speak.”

### GM Annotation — present location (998.M41) `[NEW CANON]`

Section VI records the *official* protocol — Citadel Obscura sealed, no extraction authorized. Harridan's covert program (§IV) overrode it: to run the reanimation and Rift-Travel work, the shard was extracted from Citadel Obscura and relocated up into the facility's **Null-Seal Crypt**, where it remains as of the present. The two passages are not a contradiction — they are Harridan's duplicity in a single document, hands-off in the record and hands-on in the lab.

The **anchor is unaffected.** The shard's grip is on Luna Tenebris's *mass*; while the crystal sits anywhere within the moon — Citadel or Crypt — the Wraith stays in realspace. Only removal from the moon entirely would break it.

**Endgame staging:** the [[World Lore/Characters/Dark Adeptus Mechanicus/The Body|Dark Mechanicus]] (§V, "multiple cults attempting to breach containment") are working to carry the shard *back down* to Citadel Obscura to complete its ascension. When the players reach the Null-Seal Crypt, the shard is already moving — the chase runs from the Crypt into the hulk.
