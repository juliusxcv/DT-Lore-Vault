## **Inquisitorial Report – Space Hulk Designate: "Midnight Wraith"**

**Document Code:** IX-XN/SH-998.88-VX 
**Classification:** Omega-Level Anomaly – Quarantined Asset 
**Filed By:** Magos-Explorator Thalor Vex, Ordo Xenos 
**Verified By:** [[Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan]] 
**Status:** Active – Containment Incomplete

### I. **General Overview**

The _Midnight Wraith_ is a macro-agglomeration of voidcraft wreckage, derelict vessels, and lost Mechanicus constructs fused together over millennia. It exhibits standard space hulk characteristics: erratic warp emergence, internal gravity zones, and partial atmosphere retention. The hulk's mass exceeds 14.2 cubic kilometers, with over 300 identifiable hull fragments spanning multiple technological epochs.

Unlike a standard hulk, the _Midnight Wraith_ does **not** drift with the warp's tides. It is embedded deep below the crater basin near [[Research Facility Tenebris]], within the core of _[[Luna Tenebris]]_, and it *resists* the warp rather than riding it—a condition without precedent in Ordo Xenos records. It materialised within the Bellum Eternum system in **M40.220**, during the final years of the moon's mining colony.

### II. **Seal-Breach and Rift-Travel**

- **Location:** Undisclosed—prior to the hulk's arrival at Luna Tenebris
- **Date:** M40.218
- **Incident Summary:**
    - An Inquisitorial expedition under [[Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan]], accompanied by an Adeptus Astartes element *(identity redacted from the official record)*, penetrated the hulk to its deepest segment—a structure of non-Imperial origin designated **[[Citadel Obscura]]**.
    - Within the citadel, the expedition breached a containment seal composed of salt and ash, arranged in a circular barrier.
    - This act released a dormant entity referred to in recovered texts as the **Dark Soul**, or a **Shard thereof**.
    - The entity initiated **Rift-Travel**, a non-warp transdimensional propulsion method, in an attempt to return to its origin universe.
    - The expedition extracted as the hulk began to show signs of warp entry.

### III. **Materialisation at Luna Tenebris**

The hulk attempted to complete its rift-jump. It did not.

The **Shard of the Dark Soul** aboard the hulk does not want the warp—it wants to remain in real matter space. As the hulk strained toward the rift, the Shard anchored itself against the pull, clawing into the nearest deep-gravity mass and halting the jump short. In **M40.220** the _Midnight Wraith_ was dragged out of transit and embedded within the crust of _[[Luna Tenebris]]_, below what would later become [[Research Facility Tenebris]]. The impact tore open the cave network the miners would name [[The Maw]], and broke the colony within two years.

The Wraith is therefore a body at war with itself: an engine straining to leave, a passenger refusing to go. Every measurable anomaly—the local gravity distortions, the time dilation, the hulk's refusal to drift—is a symptom of that unresolved tension. See [[Shard]].

*(Re-exposure: the hulk lay dark and undetected beneath the moon for over a thousand years, until the [[Project HELIOS SHEPHERD]] weapons test breached the strata inside the facility in 995.M41, opening an uncontained wound into it and triggering the Inquisitorial response. See [[Project HELIOS SHEPHERD]].)*

### V. **Containment Protocols**

- **Citadel Obscura** sealed under Protocol Umbra-Null. The original xenos seal (salt and ash) was broken once by the expedition; Imperial containment is now overlaid atop it. It must not be broken again.
- **Midnight Wraith** tethered via orbital anchors and subterranean stabilizers
- **No extraction attempts authorized**
- **All personnel entering the hulk must undergo post-mission psychic screening and memory purge**
- **Inquisitor Harridan's research logs remain classified and inaccessible**

### VI. **Strategic Recommendations**

- **Destruction of the hulk is not viable.** Nova Cannon simulations indicate insufficient yield.
- **Continued surveillance required.** Psychic resonance increasing at unpredictable intervals.
