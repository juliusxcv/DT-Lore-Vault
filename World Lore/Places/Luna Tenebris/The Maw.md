The Maw is the name of a vast underground network of caves, dubbed so by the colony's miners. These caves were torn open in **M40.220**, when the [[Midnight Wraith]] materialised within [[Luna Tenebris]]—the event that broke the mining colony and drove its abandonment (M40.222–M40.229).
