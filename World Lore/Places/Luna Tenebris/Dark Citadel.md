## Dark Citadel

The **Dark Citadel** is the non-Imperial structure buried in the deepest segment of the [[Midnight Wraith]]. Its Imperial designation is **Citadel Obscura**; the name "Dark Citadel" is the one used in older and non-sanctioned sources. It is the vault of the [[Shard]] of the Dark Soul, and the endgame location of the campaign.

The citadel materialised *with* the hulk — it is not native to Luna Tenebris. When the [[Midnight Wraith]] was dragged out of transit and anchored into the moon's crust in M40.220, the citadel came down with it, embedded deep below the crater basin near [[Research Facility Tenebris]]. It is not part of [[The Maw]]; the Maw is the miner-named cave network the materialisation tore open, and it lies between the surface descent and the hulk itself.

### Architecture
Non-Euclidean and of unknown origin. Scans return anomalous energy signatures, geometry that does not resolve, and psychic interference consistent with xeno-archaeotech corruption. At its core is a sealed chamber holding a crystalline mass — the Shard.

### The Seals
Three layers of containment, laid in three different eras. *(Layered reading proposed to reconcile the two accounts in [[Shard]] and [[Midnight Wraith]] — [NEW CANON — needs ratification].)*

1. **The Ancient Enemy's binding** — the deepest and oldest seal, set by the entity Shard-lore names the *Ancient Enemy* during a conflict predating the Great Crusade. This is the true binding that keeps the Shard from full manifestation. It **remains partially intact**. Breaking it fully is the endgame stakes — the Dark Soul's rebirth.
2. **The salt-and-ash ward** — a circular containment barrier overlaid on the deeper seal. This is the one [[Lord Inquisitor Lucius Harridan]]'s expedition breached, in M40.218. Breaking it did not free the Shard, but it *woke* it — enough to trigger Rift-Travel and begin active influence. This is the "seal broken once — it must not be broken again."
3. **Protocol Umbra-Null** — modern Imperial containment (psychic dampeners, purity wards, anti-warp lattice), overlaid after Harridan seized Tenebris in 995.M41. The weakest and most recent layer.

### Role in the Campaign
This is the final descent and the boss arena. It is also the **Name-tier ceiling lift**: the Dark Soul may only be *named* here, at the citadel — never earlier. The Fallen ([[The Green Angel]]) can name the anomaly on the approach, but the identity-level reveal belongs to this room. The Deathwing expedition that broke the salt-and-ash ward came *for* the Fallen; he was left aboard and rode the hulk down, and the campaign's confession/confrontation thread closes here.
