Author: Helbrecht Nullis

Dear Honorable Inquisitor Seraphina,

We fought off the hordes. Masses of undead were this time accompanied by mutated foes. I charged into battle directly facing the enemy, while most others hid in the safety of the Lunar base. Cowards! Except Isabella - she bravely stared the mutations in the face and pulled the trigger. As the deformed fell, others also charged outside, no longer fearing for their lives. Everyone but the witch, who cowardly hid in the keep.

As we looted their bodies and analyzed them, we found new information about them which shall aid us in their further annihilation. I doubt we've seen the last of them.

As we gathered information on the terminals, we received intel from the previous Kill Team. They breached the perimeters and secured the Blackstar. I could hear the conviction in their deep voices - all loyal men with only one goal in mind: to enact the will of the Emperor. A shame we lost them.

I am still conflicted about working with the witch - not only is her magic a heresy, but it seems strange things are happening around her each time she casts her forbidden wizardry. To add to that, it seems she shouted something into the woods, as if she's contacting an entity invisible to us. I strongly urge you to reconsider letting her feel the blades of my chainsword - she may turn out to be a liability rather than an asset to the Emperor.

I will, however, abide by your wishes, as I trust the Emperor will guide you towards the right decision, as he does all of his believers.

Hellbrecht Nullis, Lunar Base Tenebris
