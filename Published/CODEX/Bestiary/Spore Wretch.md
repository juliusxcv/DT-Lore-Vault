## LVL 1 Autopsy:
Initial examination classified the specimen as an unusually large fungal growth native to neither Luna Tenebris nor any catalogued Imperial ecosystem. The organism possesses a bulbous cap-like structure supported by multiple tendril appendages that function as both locomotor and anchoring organs. Surface tissue exhibits characteristics consistent with fungal mycelium, including spore-producing nodules and extensive nutrient absorption membranes.

The classification proved premature.

Despite its appearance, no true fungal cells were identified during tissue sampling. Instead, the organism consists entirely of highly modified biological tissue arranged to imitate fungal morphology.


## LVL 2 Autopsy:
The specimen continuously produces microscopic spore clouds from pores embedded beneath the upper mantle. These spores display unusual biological aggression, rapidly colonizing organic matter and inducing localized tissue necrosis. Laboratory animals exposed to concentrated samples exhibited respiratory failure, hallucinations, and uncontrolled cellular mutation within hours.

More concerning is the apparent purpose of the spores.

Rather than reproducing the organism directly, they appear to function as environmental preparation mechanisms—softening biological material and rendering it suitable for later consumption or assimilation.

## LVL 3 Autopsy:
Detailed dissection revealed a dense network of nerve fibers concealed beneath the upper mantle. Initially mistaken for root-like structures, these filaments converge into a centralized processing organ occupying nearly forty percent of the organism's internal mass.

Electrical activity within this network is highly organized and displays patterns inconsistent with fungal or plant-like life. Signal transmission speeds exceed those of most known terrestrial organisms, and multiple sections continue exchanging information even after physical separation.

Most unexpectedly, the neural architecture resembles neither human nor mutant biology.

It resembles a **synaptic relay organism**.

## LVL 4 Autopsy:
Comparative analysis against restricted Ordo Xenos archives has produced a deeply troubling conclusion.

The internal synaptic structures display strong similarities to biological systems observed within **Tyranid synapse organisms**. While incomplete and heavily mutated by some unknown exposure, the architecture serves an identical purpose: receiving, amplifying, and distributing command signals across subordinate organisms.

The implication is profound.

The Spore Wretch is not merely a mutant lifeform created by Tenebris experimentation. It appears to be a failed—or perhaps evolving—attempt at creating an artificial synaptic organism through the interaction of some unknown resonance and Tyranid genetic material recovered during earlier research programs.

Whether this connection emerged intentionally or spontaneously remains unknown.
